# Future roadmap

The current release scope is the durable notification core, Slack investigation dispatch, and Email investigation dispatch (Phases 1–3). The following capabilities are deferred and are not included in this release.

## Human Feedback

- Acknowledge
- Mark candidate not related
- Confirm cause
- Confirm resolution
- Correction/revoke
- Mute/unmute
- Reopen

## Resolution Memory

- Use confirmed outcomes as explainable local historical guidance.
- No black-box learning.
- Deterministic evidence remains primary.
